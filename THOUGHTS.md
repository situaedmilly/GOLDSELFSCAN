GOLDMICRO THOUGHTS SURFACE

Purpose: persisted cognitive observations, hypotheses, questions, corrections, and research notes.

Allowed writers:
- GPTSELF
- OTHERGBTSELF
- human operator

Thought records may be written on branch THOUGHTS.

INVARIANTS
THOUGHTS != AUTHORITY
THOUGHTS != EXECUTION
THOUGHTS != TRADE SIGNAL
THOUGHTS != VERIFIED FACT

A thought becomes evidence only after explicit validation against an admitted source. A thought may propose a schema mutation, but proposal != mutation.

Recommended thought filename:
thoughts/YYYY-MM-DD/<writer>/<sequence>.md

Each thought should identify:
- writer
- timestamp
- subject
- observation
- hypothesis
- evidence references
- uncertainty
- proposed next action
