# GOLDMICRO THOUGHTS BRANCH

This branch is the writable cognitive surface for GPTSELF, OTHERGBTSELF, and human research observations.

## Invariants

THOUGHTS != AUTHORITY  
THOUGHTS != EXECUTION  
THOUGHTS != TRADE SIGNAL  
THOUGHTS != VERIFIED FACT

Thoughts may contain observations, hypotheses, classifications, corrections, research questions, and proposed mutations. They do not themselves mutate canonical doctrine or authorize market execution.

## Record contract

Each thought should identify:
- writer
- timestamp
- subject
- observation
- hypothesis
- evidence references
- uncertainty
- proposed next action

Recommended path:
`thoughts/YYYY-MM-DD/<writer>/<sequence>.md`

Example:
`thoughts/2026-09-30/GPTSELF/001.md`
