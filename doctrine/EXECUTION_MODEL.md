TWO TIMELINES

A. TRADER TIMELINE
entry timestamp -> entry price -> direction -> exit timestamp -> exit price -> realized result

B. MARKET TIMELINE
-25m -> -20m -> -15m -> -10m -> -5m -> -1m -> entry -> +1m ... +25m

The reconstruction compares these timelines without conflating them.

LAST VALID CANDLE

EARLY
Entry precedes the declared valid admission and experiences adverse excursion before qualifying displacement.

VALID
Entry occurs inside the declared admission window and qualifying displacement follows within the declared horizon.

LATE
Qualifying displacement was already underway before entry and remaining excursion is materially reduced or exhaustion is already present.

These labels are hypotheses until measured against declared predicates.
